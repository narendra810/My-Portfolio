FriendlyNeighborhood

A local marketplace designed around a simple idea: useful things are often sitting unused nearby while someone else is looking for exactly the same thing.

React Native Expo Flask MySQL

The idea

FriendlyNeighborhood is a neighbourhood-focused platform for people who want to buy, sell, lend or find useful products within a practical distance from where they live.

The idea came from a simple problem: sometimes you need something for a short period of time, but buying a new one makes little sense when someone nearby may already have it.

Instead of treating a marketplace as one giant pool of listings, FriendlyNeighborhood puts locality at the centre of the experience.

Built around location

Location is one of the core ideas behind the application. Users can work within a defined search radius so that products from extremely distant sellers do not overwhelm the experience.

The radius-based approach makes the marketplace more practical for everyday exchanges where distance matters.

Core features

Buy & sell

Users can list products they want to sell and discover products offered by other people in their area.

Borrow locally

The concept also supports situations where purchasing an item is unnecessary because someone nearby may be willing to lend it.

Radius-based discovery

Product discovery can be constrained by a user's chosen location radius, keeping results relevant to their physical surroundings.

Smart suggestions

The application can use location and product information to surface items that are more relevant to the user, rather than simply presenting an undifferentiated list.

Under the hood

The application is being developed as a mobile-first experience using React Native and Expo, with a Flask backend and MySQL for persistent data.

The project is still under development, so the current version should be considered a working prototype rather than a finished production marketplace.
